Why It Matters

Blood can't be manufactured — it can only come from someone like you.

Every two seconds, someone needs blood. Hospitals rely entirely on voluntary donors to treat accident victims, surgery patients, cancer patients, and mothers during childbirth. There is no substitute — only donors.

Whole Blood

Takes about 10 minutes. Replenished by your body within 24–48 hours. Donatable every 12 weeks.

Platelets

Critical for cancer patients and surgery. Takes 1–2 hours via apheresis, donatable every 7 days.

Plasma

Used in emergency and trauma care. Takes about 45 minutes, donatable every 28 days.

Before You Register

Am I eligible to donate?

  • Aged between 18 and 65 years
  • Weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs)
  • Hemoglobin level of 12.5 g/dL or higher
  • No donation in the last 12 weeks
  • Generally in good health, free of infection
  • No tattoos or piercings in the last 6 months
